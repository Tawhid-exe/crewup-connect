# CrewUp Connect

Web Application Project Proposal (MERN Stack)

1. Project Title

CrewUp – A Community-Driven Volunteer Management and Event Platform

2. Problem Statement

Many community organizations, NGOs, and student clubs struggle to recruit and manage volunteers efficiently. Most volunteer opportunities are currently shared through social media. Social media posts can easily be missed which results in low participation and poor coordination. Additionally, there is no centralized platform where volunteers can discover opportunities, track their contributions, and showcase their volunteering experience.

Stakeholders affected -

● Volunteers who are looking for meaningful community service opportunity.

● NGOs and community organizations that need volunteers.

● Student clubs and event organizers managing volunteer-based events

Current Challenges -

● Volunteer recruitment is heavily dependent on social media

● Individuals lack a streamlined way to discover opportunities for voluntary works based on their specific interests, location, and availability

● Difficulty managing volunteer registrations, attendance and event operations.

● No verified record of volunteer hours or participation

Why Existing Solutions are Insufficient -

● Existing methods lack a centralized system for volunteer management, event organization, attendance tracking, certificate generation, and maintaining verified volunteer profiles. Also, General social media platforms do not provide tools for individuals to discover opportunities based on their specific interests, location, and availability. As a result, both volunteers and organizations face inefficiencies in coordinating community service activities.

3. Proposed Solution

CrewUp is a MERN Stack web application that connects volunteers with NGOs, student clubs, and community organizations through a single online platform. Organizations can create and manage volunteer events, while individuals can search for opportunities based on their interests, location, and availability. Volunteers can register for events, track volunteer hours, earn digital certificates, and build a verified volunteer portfolio.

Target Users –

● Volunteers

● NGOs

● Community Organizations

● Student Clubs

● Event Organizers

Key benefits -

● Easy discovery of volunteer opportunities

● Efficient event and volunteer management for organizations

● Verified volunteer profiles with participation history

● Digital certificates and volunteer portfolios

● Increased community engagement through a centralized platform

4. Features & Functional Requirements

User Features -

● User registration and login

● Complete and manage personal profile

● Search and register for volunteer events

● Track volunteer contribution and hours

Organizer Features -

● Create and manage volunteer events

● Manage registered volunteers

● Approve volunteer participation.

● Provide digital certificates

Admin Features -

● Verify organizations

● Remove fake or inappropriate events

● Admin dashboard
